## 🏛️ Free Trade, By Choice

**Let's be honest:** Transactions and contracts are regulated. Laws exist. We're not here to fight that fight.

What we're building is different.

---

## 🎯 PhoenixPME is a CHOICE.

A choice to trade with people who value:

✅ **Integrity over anonymity** – TRUST tokens are earned, not bought  
✅ **Accountability over pseudonymity** – DONT TRUST tokens follow bad actors  
✅ **Direct trade over intermediaries** – No banks, no dealers, no 15% fees  
✅ **Code over promises** – Smart contracts don't freeze accounts  

This isn't a rebellion. It's an **alternative**.

Use regulated channels if you want. Or use PhoenixPME if you want:

- Both parties post collateral (skin in the game)
- Reputation that's permanent and earned
- Community governance, not corporate control

**The gatekeepers built walls. We're building a door.**

**You choose which side you stand on.**
